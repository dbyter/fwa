Early-warning logic panel
Display the rule or model criteria that generated the alert.
Example:
Alert generated when at least three of the following persisted for two consecutive months:
Above the 99th peer percentile for allowed dollars per beneficiary.
More than fivefold six-month growth.
More than 70% of billing concentrated in three HCPCS codes.
More than 20 states added within six months.
More than 60% of orders associated with five clinicians.
Significant overlap with a previously adverse supplier network.
Example narrative
This supplier crossed the retrospective review threshold 17 months before revocation. At that point, the claims data already showed rapid growth, a highly concentrated recurring-supply code mix, broad geographic expansion, and dependence on a small group of ordering clinicians.
Important caveat
The earliest statistical deviation should not automatically be presented as the appropriate intervention date. The visual should distinguish:
First weak signal.
First sustained signal.
First operationally credible alert.
